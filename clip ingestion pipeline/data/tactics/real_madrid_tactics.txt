=== REAL MADRID — PRE-MATCH TACTICAL BRIEF (SAMPLE) ===
Opponent: (generic top-half side)   Competition: La Liga   Venue: Home (Bernabéu)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
FORMATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Base shape: 4-3-3, morphing to 4-2-3-1 in build-up and a 3-2-5 in
settled attack (full-back inverts to form a double pivot).

   GK
   RB    RCB   LCB   LB
        DM (single pivot)
   RCM(8)        LCM(8 / advanced)
   RW       ST(9)       LW
- LB tucks inside (inverted full-back) next to the DM → back-three + double pivot.
- RB stays higher/wider to give right-sided width and overlap.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY PLAYER ROLES (sample)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- No. 9 (striker): plays on the last shoulder, pins the centre-backs,
  makes channel runs in behind; first defender in the press.
- Left winger: 1v1 isolation specialist, hugs the touchline, attacks
  the byline for cutbacks; stay HIGH and WIDE to stretch their block.
- Advanced 8 (left central mid): late runs into the box, third-man
  combinations, arrives in the "second wave" — be the +1 in the box.
- Single pivot (DM): screen the back line, set the tempo, no. 6 must
  always offer a passing angle behind the opponent's first press line.
- Right-back: provide overlapping width and the "rest-attack" outlet on
  the far side for switches of play.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
IN POSSESSION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Build patiently from the back; goalkeeper as the +1 to beat the
   first press. Split the centre-backs, drop the pivot if needed.
2. Attack the HALF-SPACES (the lanes between full-back and centre-back).
   Overload one side, then SWITCH the play to the isolated winger.
3. Create 2v1 and 3v2 overloads out wide; full-back + winger + 8 form
   wide triangles to progress through the thirds.
4. Tempo: quick one- and two-touch combinations through the middle
   third (tiki-taka style triangles). AVOID low-percentage long balls
   unless the far-side winger is genuinely isolated 1v1.
5. Goal threat: cutbacks from the byline to the penalty spot; late-
   arriving runners from midfield; back-post crashing from far winger.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
OUT OF POSSESSION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. High press / gegenpressing: win the ball back within 6 seconds of
   losing it (counter-press). Front three set the pressing traps.
2. Press triggers: a back-pass, a heavy first touch, or a pass into a
   player facing his own goal → close down immediately, force the turnover.
3. Mid-block 4-4-2 if the press is broken: stay compact, protect the
   central lanes, force play into wide areas (the "press them outside").
4. Rest defence: keep a back-three + pivot behind the ball at all times
   to kill counter-attacks before they start.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
TRANSITIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Attack → Defence (loss of ball): nearest man presses instantly; the
  rest drop and cover central passing lanes to deny the quick counter.
- Defence → Attack (regain): play forward fast — first pass breaks a
  line; striker + wingers attack the space in behind before they reset.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SET PIECES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Attacking corners: near-post flick-on run + two runners attacking the
  6-yard box; one designated edge-of-box "second ball" recycler.
- Defending: zonal + man-marking hybrid; protect the near post and
  the penalty spot; quick outlet to the winger on the clearance.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
NON-NEGOTIABLES (the game plan in one breath)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
High press, win it back in 6 seconds, dominate the half-spaces, keep
width on the wings, switch play to the isolated winger, and always keep
a rest-defence behind the ball.
