=== AI SOCCER COACHING ASSISTANT — ACTIVE GAME PLAN ===
Tactical Philosophy: High-Pressing Positional Play (Tik-Taka / Guardiola Influence)

Core Guidelines:
1. High Defensive Pressing (Out of Possession):
   - Immediately press the opponent when possession is lost in the opponent's half (the "6-second rule").
   - Restrict opponent passing channels. Form compact defensive triangles around the ball carrier (crowding / double-teaming).
   - If an opponent midfielder receives the ball facing their own goal, press intensely to force a turnover.

2. Positional Structure & Passing (In Possession):
   - Maintain width on the wings to stretch the opponent's defensive block.
   - Look for rapid short passes (triangulations) through the middle third. Avoid long low-percentage balls unless a winger is completely isolated.
   - The ball-carrier must always have at least two passing options (left/right supporting triangles).

3. Turnovers & Defensive Transitions:
   - When we lose possession, the nearest player must press immediately, while the rest drop to cover central passing lanes to prevent quick counter-attacks.
   - Minimize unforced passing turnovers in the defensive and middle thirds.
